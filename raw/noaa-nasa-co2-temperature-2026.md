## Global temperature (NASA GISTEMP)
- 2025 annual global surface temperature anomaly: +1.19 °C (+2.14 °F) relative to the 1951–1980 average.
- 2024 is the hottest year on record (since 1880). 2025 was cooler than 2024 and effectively tied with 2023. The 10 most recent years are the warmest on record.
- Not stated: warming per decade; anomaly relative to pre-industrial or 20th-century average.
