# NOAA JetStream — Atmosphere layers & composition (notes)

Five layers distinguished by temperature, composition, movement, density; each bounded by a "pause".

## Troposphere
- Surface to ~18–20 km (11–12 mi) at equator, ~9 km (5.5 mi) at 50°N/S, just under 6 km (4 mi) at poles.
- Temperature falls with height: avg ~17°C (62°F) at surface to −51°C (−60°F) at tropopause.
- Almost all weather occurs here. Top boundary: tropopause.

## Stratosphere
- From ~6–20 km up to ~50 km (31 mi).
- Temperature rises with height: −51°C → ~−15°C (5°F) at top; warming from heat released during ozone formation.
- Holds ~19% of the atmosphere's gases, very little water vapor.
- Warm over cool → convection suppressed; explains flattened anvil tops of cumulonimbus. Top boundary: stratopause.

## Mesosphere
- ~50–85 km (31–53 mi). Temperature decreases with height (≈ −15°C near bottom); top is coldest.
- Meteors burn up here. Top boundary: mesopause.

## Thermosphere
- ~85–600 km (53–375 mi). Temperature rises from ~−120°C to as high as ~2,000°C (3,600°F) near top; absorbs high-energy UV and X-rays. Molecules are sparse, so it would still feel cold.

## Ionosphere (within thermosphere per the ionosphere page)
- ~60–300 km (37–190 mi). Ionized gas created by solar radiation. D, E, F layers; denser by day; D layer essentially disappears at night; F splits in two by day.
- Marconi's 1901 transatlantic signal implied a conducting layer ~100 km up; Appleton named it the E-layer in 1927. Skywaves bounce radio back to Earth.

## Exosphere
- ~600–10,000 km (375–6,200 mi). Atoms/molecules escape to space; satellites orbit here. Base boundary: thermopause.

## Composition of dry air
- N2 78.084%, O2 20.946% (another table: 20.947%), Ar 0.934%, CO2 0.042%. Top four = 99.998%.
- ppm: Ne 18.182, He 5.24, CH4 1.92, Kr 1.14, H2 0.55, N2O 0.33, CO 0.10, Xe 0.09, O3 0.07, NO2 0.02, I 0.01, NH3 trace.
- Water vapor: up to ~4% by volume (near 0 in deserts, ~3% on hot humid days, ~4% in tropics). At 4% vapor: N2 ~74.96%, O2 ~20.11%, Ar ~0.89%.
- Pressure values are not given on these pages.
