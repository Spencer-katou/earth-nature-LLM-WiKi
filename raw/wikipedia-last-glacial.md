# Last glacial period & LGM (notes)

## Last Glacial Period
- From the end of the Last Interglacial to the start of the Holocene: about 115,000–11,700 years ago (most of the Late Pleistocene).
- Regional names: Wisconsin (N. America), Weichselian (N. Europe), Würm (Alps), Devensian (Britain), etc.
- Laurentide Ice Sheet covered almost all of Canada and the northern US; Scandinavian ice reached northern Britain, Germany, Poland, Russia. Alaska mostly ice-free.
- Taiwan was repeatedly glaciated 44,250–10,680 years BP (mountain glaciers).
- Younger Dryas cooling ~12,800–11,700 years ago (LGM page summary: 12,900); its end marks the end of the glacial period and start of the Holocene (~11,700 years ago).

## Last Glacial Maximum (LGM)
- Most recent time of greatest ice-sheet extent: between about 26,000 and 20,000 years ago. No agreed start/end dates; e.g. onset 27,500 years ago, maximum by ~26,000, deglaciation from 20,000–19,000 years ago (Jennifer French); Southern Hemisphere maximum 26,500–20,000 years ago.
- Global temperature about 6 °C colder than today around 21,000 years ago.
- Sea level about 125 m lower ~21,000 years ago (another caption: more than 110 m lower at 20,000 years ago). South China Sea ~100 m lower.
- Ice covered ~8% of Earth's surface and 25% of land (today ~3.1% and 10.7%).
- [S] Laurentide volume 26.5–37 million km³, max height ~3.2 km.
- Exposed land: Sundaland (Borneo, Bali joined to Asia), Sahul (Australia–New Guinea–Tasmania), Doggerland (Britain–Jutland); [S] Bering land bridge. Taiwan Strait not mentioned.
- Atmospheric dust 20–25× present; some regions had up to 90% less rainfall.
- CO2 at LGM: not found in text read.

## Deglaciation
- Northern warming began ~20,000 years ago but limited; considerable warming ~14,600 years ago (Bølling–Allerød); abrupt sea-level rise ~14,500 years ago (Meltwater pulse 1A).
